# L*=10 Professional ATP Smoke Test — 60-Second Breakpoint

This branch triggers 20 frozen L*=10 ocean instances for Vampire, E Prover, SPASS, and Prover9.

Each solver process receives a 60-second internal limit where supported and is also killed by the benchmark harness at 60 seconds. Generated instances are BFS-verified at L*=10 before execution. Raw outputs and environment/version data are retained as artifacts.
